Version 1.2.0 improves weather consistency and reliability.

- Pebble weather is tried first, with automatic Open-Meteo fallback
- 15-minute refreshes, two-minute retries after missed updates and refresh on reconnect
- HTTP timeouts, response validation and AppMessage delivery retries
- Native Fahrenheit readings avoid converting already-rounded Celsius values
- Cached observations older than two hours are replaced with a placeholder
- Existing 12-hour/24-hour and Celsius/Fahrenheit settings are preserved
- Native 200 × 228 Pebble Time 2 layout
- Rounded halftone transition shaped to follow the metal frame
- Time, automatically localized weekday/date and current local temperature
- Automatic Timeline Quick View layout
- Configurable background and text colors
- Optional 4-step transition, floating shadow and edge-to-edge text
- Weather caching with observation timestamps
- Weekday translations for 17 languages with English fallback
