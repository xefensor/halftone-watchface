Halftone 1.9.0 rc.5 aligns the visible bottom of contextual artwork and notification icons with their text.

- Aligns artwork/icons with the actual fitted foreground text, including an ellipsis when needed and excluding the text shadow
- Uses compiled-font glyph measurements and accounts for transparent icon padding and empty space around fitted icons
- Replaces the previous centered-plus-offset placement without moving the clock or text
- Retains compact artwork/icon boxes: 32 pixels normally or 40 with Big text for two-line panels, and 28 pixels for a single line
- Preserves center-cropped music images, fully contained notification icons, the title inset and long-title artwork hiding
- Keeps both complications above Timeline Peek, existing source choices, the halftone dot border and notification privacy defaults

Incoming source artwork remains capped at 24 pixels. Notification summaries and message text remain off by default.

This build requires Pebble's plugin preview and is prepared for sideloaded testing. Do not submit it to the public appstore while Pebble's plugin-preview publishing restriction remains in place. Weather, units and updates come from the source selected in Pebble; Halftone has no independent weather HTTP fallback or local temperature conversion. Music and notification sources are currently available on Android.
