Version 1.1.0 adds requested time and temperature formats.

- New 12-hour time option (24-hour remains the default)
- New Fahrenheit option (Celsius remains the default)
- Format changes apply immediately from the phone settings page
- Native 200 × 228 Pebble Time 2 layout
- Rounded halftone transition shaped to follow the metal frame
- Time, automatically localized weekday/date and current local temperature
- Automatic Timeline Quick View layout
- Configurable background and text colors
- Optional 4-step transition, floating shadow and edge-to-edge text
- Weather caching and 30-minute refresh interval
- Weekday translations for 17 languages with English fallback
