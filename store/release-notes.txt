Halftone 1.9.0 rc.3 balances the clock and makes music artwork fit its panel.

- Centers the visible clock digits using compiled font bounds, rather than the font line box
- Fits the clock above active context, keeping shorter and taller times balanced and clear of the readings
- Center-crops rectangular album art to a square without stretching, then scales it to the music block: 40 pixels normally or 48 with Big text; single-line music art uses 32 pixels
- Adjusts the title inset to the displayed art and preserves artwork hiding when it lets the full title fit
- Keeps incoming source images capped at 24 pixels; notification icons remain 24 pixels
- Retains both complications above native Timeline Peek and Big text for the date, clock, readings, units and contextual text
- Preserves the halftone dot pattern, rounded border, colors, source choices and notification privacy defaults

Clock size and position adapt to available space when context changes, then remain still during the brief dissolve frames. The first two global choices in Pebble's Complications settings remain the two main readings. Notification summaries and message text remain off by default.

This build requires Pebble's plugin preview and is prepared for sideloaded testing. Do not submit it to the public appstore while Pebble's plugin-preview publishing restriction remains in place. Weather, units and updates come from the source selected in Pebble; Halftone has no independent weather HTTP fallback or local temperature conversion. Music and notification sources are currently available on Android.
