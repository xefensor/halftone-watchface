Halftone 1.9.0 rc.4 makes contextual artwork smaller and aligns notification icons with it.

- Uses matching compact boxes for music artwork and notification icons: 32 pixels normally or 40 with Big text for two-line panels, and 28 pixels for a single line
- Moves both artwork and icons 2 pixels below the panel's vertical center, leaving more breathing room above them
- Keeps rectangular album art center-cropped to a square without stretching; notification icons and generic reading images stay fully visible inside their boxes
- Preserves the title inset and artwork hiding when removing it lets the full title fit
- Keeps incoming source artwork capped at 24 pixels
- Leaves the balanced clock, accented date clearance, both complications above Timeline Peek and text sizing unchanged from rc.3
- Preserves the halftone dot pattern, rounded border, source choices and notification privacy defaults

The first two global choices in Pebble's Complications settings remain the two main readings. Notification summaries and message text remain off by default.

This build requires Pebble's plugin preview and is prepared for sideloaded testing. Do not submit it to the public appstore while Pebble's plugin-preview publishing restriction remains in place. Weather, units and updates come from the source selected in Pebble; Halftone has no independent weather HTTP fallback or local temperature conversion. Music and notification sources are currently available on Android.
