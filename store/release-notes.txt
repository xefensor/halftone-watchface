Version 1.3.1 fixes automatic weather refresh while the watchface stays open.

- Pebble weather is tried first, with automatic Open-Meteo fallback
- Fixed scheduled weather requests being ignored by the phone's message listener
- Phone-side 30-minute refresh timer backs up requests from the watch
- Stalled location, HTTP and message-delivery requests recover without reopening the face
- Normal refreshes every 30 minutes again
- Up to six hours of hourly forecasts saved on the watch in both temperature units
- Offline forecasts advance with the current hour, including across midnight
- Forecast estimates are marked with ~ and survive restarting the watchface
- Two-minute retries after missed updates and refresh on reconnect are retained
- HTTP timeouts, response validation and AppMessage delivery retries
- Native Fahrenheit readings avoid converting already-rounded Celsius values
- A placeholder appears when neither a usable forecast nor a recent observation is available
- Existing 12-hour/24-hour and Celsius/Fahrenheit settings are preserved
- Native 200 × 228 Pebble Time 2 layout
- Rounded halftone transition shaped to follow the metal frame
- Time, automatically localized weekday/date and current local temperature
- Automatic Timeline Quick View layout
- Configurable background and text colors
- Optional 4-step transition, floating shadow and edge-to-edge text
- Weather caching with observation timestamps
- Weekday translations for 17 languages with English fallback
