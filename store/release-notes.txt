Halftone 1.9.0 rc.2 improves readability in the normal, contextual and native Timeline Peek layouts.

- Keeps both selected complications side by side when Timeline Peek is visible
- Replaces the large-clock option with Big text for the date, clock, readings, units and music/notification text
- Adds larger reading text with fitting for long values and compact displays
- Refines spacing above the native timeline panel and around contextual content
- Preserves the halftone dot pattern, rounded border, colors and text shadow
- Retains existing saved text-size preferences and localized settings help

The first two global choices in Pebble's Complications settings remain the two main readings. Music and new notifications use the lower contextual panel when native Timeline Peek is closed. Notification summaries and message text remain off by default.

This build requires Pebble's plugin preview and is prepared for sideloaded testing. Do not submit it to the public appstore while Pebble's plugin-preview publishing restriction remains in place. Weather, units and updates come from the source selected in Pebble; Halftone has no independent weather HTTP fallback or local temperature conversion. Music and notification sources are currently available on Android.
