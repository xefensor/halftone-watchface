Halftone 1.9.0 rc.1 refines the layout around two large complications in balanced side-by-side columns, with a lower panel that adapts to what is happening on your phone.

- Uses the first two global choices in Pebble's Complications settings
- Keeps both readings visible in a compact row above music or new notifications
- Refined spacing, margins and text fitting across the normal and contextual layouts
- Large numbers, smaller units and a droplet for precipitation probability
- Music title and artist, with artwork hidden when that lets the whole title fit
- Optional notification summaries, app-name filters and a separate message-text setting
- Brief halftone panel transitions that can be turned off
- Native Timeline Peek support and small low-battery/connection indicators
- Updated settings translations in all 17 supported languages
- Source units, signs, decimal punctuation and zero values retained
- Existing colors, 12/24-hour time, extra-large text, shadow and dot-border options preserved

This build requires Pebble's plugin preview. Weather and other readings come from the source selected in Pebble; there is no independent weather HTTP fallback or local temperature conversion. Music and notification sources are currently available on Android. Notification summaries and message text are off by default.
