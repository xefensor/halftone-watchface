A bold Pebble clock with a halftone dot border.

Halftone puts a distinctive clock and two useful readings on your wrist. Choose weather, battery or other compatible Pebble readings, then make the face your own with colour, depth and shadow.

- Keep the time, date and two readings together.
- Customize the colours and dot-border appearance.
- Adjust depth, shadow, Bigger Text and text size.
- See a Timer countdown or music with its title, artist and available artwork.
- Choose your Activities and their order. A smaller secondary summary appears when space allows.
- Show Timeline Peek alongside the clock.

Activities are ongoing updates from other apps. Start a countdown in Timer, then return to Halftone to see the time remaining. Timer handles its controls and alarm. Open Activity can add an optional Quick Launch shortcut back to Timer.

For Pebble Time 2 and Round 2. Shared readings and Activities require a connected phone, plugin support enabled in the official Pebble phone app, and permissions for the chosen sources. Built-in music and notifications require Android. Notification summaries and message text are separate opt-in settings.

Free. Part of XOCE. No XOCE account, ads or analytics. Selected providers have their own requirements and privacy practices.
