A Pebble watchface with a bold Fira clock, a halftone dot border and room for your everyday information.

Halftone pairs distinctive typography with two readings beneath the time. Choose weather, battery or another compatible Pebble reading, then make the clock your own with colour, depth and shadow settings.

- Keep two readings together with complete values and units.
- Customize colours, Bigger Text, depth and shadow.
- Choose Small, Medium, Large or Follow Pebble text.
- Show one main activity and an optional smaller summary below the readings.
- Follow music with the song, artist and available artwork, and use Timeline Peek.

A countdown can stay in view when you leave Timer. Start it there, then return to Halftone to see the time remaining. These ongoing updates are called Activities. Timer still runs the countdown and handles the alarm. Open Timer to change it, or add Open Activity as an optional button shortcut back to the app.

Shared readings and activities need the official Pebble phone app's plugin support, source permissions and a connected phone. Music and notification integration requires Android. Notifications are optional; message text is opt-in.

Free. For Pebble Time 2 and Round 2. No account, advertisements or analytics.
