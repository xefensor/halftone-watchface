Halftone makes the color e-paper display feel like part of the Pebble Time 2 body. A precise halftone border grows out of the black bezel and curves around the screen with the same rounded shape as the metal frame.

Features:
- Large, readable digital time with 12-hour and 24-hour options
- Weekday automatically localized to the PebbleOS language
- Current local temperature in °C or °F, using Pebble weather with Open-Meteo fallback
- Six-hour offline forecast cache, with estimated readings marked by ~
- Layout that automatically adapts to Timeline Quick View
- Configurable background and text colors
- Original 3-step or denser 4-step dot transition
- Optional edge-to-edge text and floating shadow
- Minute-only clock updates for low battery use

Designed specifically for Pebble Time 2 (Emery). Fetching weather requires a connected phone, internet access and location permission. Halftone sends the coordinates requested by the Pebble phone app to Pebble's weather service, or to Open-Meteo for fallback weather or a supplemental hourly forecast. Weather refreshes every 30 minutes, retries missed updates and refreshes after reconnecting. Cached forecasts can cover up to six hours offline for the last fetched location; estimates are marked with ~. A placeholder appears after all usable weather expires. It contains no account, advertising or analytics.

Included weekday languages: English, Czech, Slovak, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Hungarian, Romanian and Turkish. Unsupported locales fall back to English.
