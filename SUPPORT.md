# Halftone support

Report issues at [Halftone issues](https://github.com/xefensor/halftone-watchface/issues). Include the app version, watch model/firmware, phone/Pebble app versions and reproduction steps. Remove private notification, music and location content.

Candidates target Time 2 and Round 2 with SDK 4.44. Round 2 checks use the emulator. Physical Time 2 and Android checks remain required before publication. Duo is deferred.
